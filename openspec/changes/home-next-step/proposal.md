# Proposal

## Why

Home showed the next lesson, but not why it matters. Students act when one clear step tells them what it gives them now.

## What Changes

- One "next step" card is the first thing under the status line, in place of the play button. It names the exercise, why it moves the student forward in one line, the reward, and what it costs (minutes, questions, whether it completes today's goal).
- The step is picked by what helps most now: an open round; a crown one right answer away; a lesson behind; the weakest lesson of a test within two weeks; the lesson that moves the forecast most toward the next league; else the next lesson.
- The home stays one screen.
